# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities to [hello@cortexbrain.ai](mailto:hello@cortexbrain.ai?subject=Security%20Report) rather than opening a public issue. Include the affected feature or URL, reproduction steps, observed impact, and a contact method for follow-up.

Researchers must avoid accessing, modifying, deleting, or disclosing other parties’ data; degrading availability; using social engineering; or publicly disclosing a report before CORTEX has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate it. This policy does not authorize activity that violates law or third-party rights.

## Handling reports

CORTEX reviews submitted reports, prioritizes confirmed issues by potential impact, and communicates with the reporter when contact information is provided. The public reporting process is also published at `/.well-known/security.txt` and `/.well-known/security-policy.txt`.

## Scope

This policy covers the public CORTEX website. Customer environments, integrations, and product deployments may have additional reporting paths in the relevant customer agreement or deployment documentation.
