# Site-Wide Footer Validation

The new Terms route opens at `/terms.html` as a clear CORTEX legal placeholder with a marked draft status and no claim that it is a published agreement. The rendered page includes the shared CORTEX footer, where Privacy and Terms appear within Field Documents and again in the legal lockup. The footer’s desktop grid is now distributed across the available canvas, with the identity section, two navigation groups, and the access action occupying distinct horizontal columns rather than forming a narrow vertical stack.
