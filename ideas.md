# CORTEX Design Direction

## Reference Ground Truth

This landing page reproduces the supplied CORTEX build brief and scroll-video experience. The supplied product footage is the visual source of truth: an appliance on a stone floor, black cables passing through a wall, and those cables assembling into a physical company brain. The page must make the user’s scroll feel like a direct, physical traversal from local compute to a protected company brain.

## Chosen Approach: Cinematic Physical Infrastructure Storytelling

**Design Movement:** Architectural minimalism with restrained Apple-style product storytelling, informed by premium industrial infrastructure rather than SaaS marketing.

**Core Principles:**

1. The supplied video is the primary product explanation; copy supports it without competing for attention.
2. Visual authority comes from material, shadow, typography, and whitespace—not neon, gradients, dashboard cards, or generic AI imagery.
3. Information is arranged as a sequential field manual: concise headers, engraved rules, technical labels, and deliberately paced sections.
4. The product boundary is made tangible through the repeated relationship of appliance, wall, cable, and brain.

**Color Philosophy:** The site is almost-black to act as a quiet gallery for the warm concrete, silver appliance, black cables, and white cable pulses within the footage. Warm off-white type provides high contrast without looking clinical; soft stone and muted bronze traces add hierarchy with no synthetic “AI” color cues.

**Layout Paradigm:** A fixed cinematic stage occupies the opening 520vh. Narrative copy sits in asymmetric left/right rails according to the visual clear space in each moment of the footage. After the final brain frame, a dark editorial document unfolds in full-width slabs, rules, offsets, and comparison columns rather than a repeated card grid.

**Signature Elements:** A hairline reference grid and small coordinate ticks; restrained monospaced uppercase labels made from Inter; an inline signal line/“conscience pulse” that appears in buttons, section rules, and the technical diagram.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Scrolling is an input device, not navigation chrome. The scroll-scrubbed product video follows the user’s pace, while content appears with quiet opacity and vertical-reveal transitions. Links and buttons respond with brief, tactile changes in tone and a small directional shift only.

**Animation:** Video time is interpolated against scroll position; text reveals use 700ms cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) opacity and 32px upward translation with small stagger offsets. The logo and signal line remain still except for soft hover or focus states. Reduced-motion users see poster imagery and direct transitions rather than scroll scrubbing.

**Typography System:** Inter is the sole family. Large headlines use 600–700 weight with tight tracking and deliberate line breaks; reading copy is 400 with generous leading; metadata uses 500–600 uppercase with 0.16–0.22em tracking. The visual tone is architectural and precise rather than playful.

**Brand Essence:** CORTEX is a governed company brain that runs inside the boundary where the company’s knowledge already lives. **Physical, assured, exacting.**

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are declarative and spare; labels are technical and grounded; CTAs invite a serious next conversation rather than offer vague product discovery. Example lines: “Nothing listens from outside.” and “Run the intelligence where the information stays.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** The wordmark is set as a compact, custom-spaced CORTEX label paired with a square cortical loop mark: an ivory C-shaped cable passage suggesting a protected brain and a wall boundary. No generic font-only logo treatment.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Conscience Ivory — #EEE9DD**, a warm, material white that belongs against the project’s near-black infrastructure ground.

## Style Decisions

- Every cinematic story beat must retain a visible material anchor from the supplied appliance, wall, cables, or finished brain, paired with a quiet signal cue rather than a generic overlay.
- CORTEX identity is always carried by the cortical-loop mark, custom-spaced wordmark, coordinate ticks, or the conscience-pulse signal line; it is never treated as a plain isolated text label.
- Technical-document sections alternate information-dense editorial fields with supplied material proof imagery so the full page remains grounded in physical infrastructure.
- Primary imagery centers the supplied appliance, boundary, cable, company-brain, manifest, and record vocabulary; any archival glyph remains a tightly bounded supporting act rather than the page-wide visual language.
- Headlines remain tight, declarative infrastructure statements. Broad SaaS challenges and poster-like AI slogans are excluded.
- The cortical-loop mark, coordinate ticks, and conscience-pulse line recur in navigation, diagrams, section rules, and CTAs as the continuous CORTEX identity system.
- Edge-sheet technical annotations attach to a tangible appliance, wall boundary, sealed chassis, cable route, or local company-brain material; abstract radial graphics remain subordinate texture rather than the page-wide visual subject.
- Values & Culture centers a restrained engraved clasped-hands gesture as the material proof of shared momentum; editorial scale, linework, and motion carry the rest of the page rather than a gallery of images.
- Values & Culture treats speed as a verified operating cadence inside a trust boundary; each entry carries a tangible CORTEX signal, verification, pairing, or coordinate cue rather than a generic startup-manifesto device.
- The One-Pager repeats the local route—sealed appliance, wall boundary, outbound cable, protected brain—through its hero, document annotations, and closing lockup rather than treating infrastructure as a hero-only visual.
- One-Pager editorial fields use asymmetric rails, coordinate-like labels, conscience-pulse traces, and compact material proof so the document reads as an authored CORTEX artifact rather than a centered SaaS poster.
- The One-Pager contents rail carries a cortical-loop marker and cable tick; dark material slabs punctuate freshness and economics so its physical-infrastructure authority is preserved inside a lighter document field.
- Every One-Pager proof field is instrumented with a local-route annotation, boundary cue, or measured signal rather than empty outline cards or text-only claims.
- The explicit light-mode alternate renders supplied imagery in monochrome on a black-on-white technical field sheet, with olive reserved for measured signal accents rather than large color fields.
- In light mode, the CORTEX identity repeats through small olive field traces, coordinate marks, and quiet conscience-pulse rules around major proof sections.
