# Original Cortex Motion Adaptation Map

The original Cortex homepage builds its story as a sequence of full-viewport acts rather than a conventional scroll document. It pairs sparse uppercase copy with radial ray fields, a central engraving or system object, then moves the focus from memory to governance to proof. The appliance stage transitions from a single node to an estate, and the final access stage resolves into an oversized CORTEX mark.

The CORTEX adaptation will preserve its existing supplied video and use the same principles rather than copy the original site directly: reversible stage progression, a breathing radial field, source-token reveal, movement along the appliance-estate paths, staged evidence copy, and a final brand-mark arrival. All treatments remain warm ivory on near-black rather than violet.
